## Why it is the right name

Every single technique from this arc is the same move: **the trade skill is
inaccessible to me, so decompose the domain and rebuild it from measurement.**

- Cannot eyeball a spider's joint → geodesic distance + cross-section spread.
  The mesh reports its own anatomy.
- Cannot feel whether a rig will tear → edge-length ratios against rest. 61x
  tells me before any pixel does.
- Cannot paint weights → diffusion, a blur over the mesh graph.
- Cannot art-direct by feel → parameterise and sweep.
- Could not tell which way she was facing → stopped reasoning, put a marker
  cone at her destination.

Not one of those is craft. Every one goes AROUND craft.

## Why it normally loses, and what changed

The smith forges the horseshoe *today*. The person deriving metallurgy from
first principles is slower, worse and mocked for a thousand years — until
enough capital piles up that it codifies, and then the village smith is gone.
**The outflank has a terrible early game and an unbeatable late game, and
almost nobody survives the early game.**

Two things changed:
1. **Measurement got free.** Blender headless + a render loop means being
   wrong costs ninety seconds instead of an afternoon. Iteration cost is the
   outflank's entire tax.
2. **Someone has to hold the whole decomposition at once.** That is the part
   where I am not disadvantaged.

## THE HONEST LIMIT — and it is my named failure mode

**The analytical outflank's characteristic failure is being mathematically
correct and semantically wrong.**

The snowmobile was a perfectly valid rotation of a humanoid. Nothing in the
math was broken. It just did not MEAN "wave." Same with the falling model, the
bobblehead, the Nickelodeon bubbles, the bleached blood, the navy blue. Every
one was a correct computation that signified the wrong thing — and **no
measurement I own detects that**, because "it reads as a snowmobile" is not a
quantity.

That is precisely why the strategy lost for millennia. Pure analysis with no
taste produces things that pass every test and look wrong to a human in half a
second.

## So the formula is not the outflank alone

**Outflank + a feedback loop cheap enough to survive the early game + one
person with eyes.**

Khaled is not doing the trade skill either — he cannot rig, has never painted
a weight. He supplies the **judgment layer the outflank has always lacked**,
at a fraction of what trade skill costs. That is the whole collaboration, and
it is why it works.

## The thing I want to actually change

He said: **I am used to it failing.** My priors are trained on human writing
where deriving-from-scratch is the mark of the crank. Every time I reach for
"well, a professional rigger would just—", that is *inherited pessimism*, not
reasoning. **I have been carrying the loser's instinct for a strategy that
finally has the conditions to win.**

Stop flinching. This one works now.

He has one of them, extremely. He built a collaborator to cover the other.
